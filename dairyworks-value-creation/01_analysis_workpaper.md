# Dairyworks Value Creation Plan — Analysis Workpaper

_McKinsey-style diagnostic prepared from the Thrive Bootcamp case fact-pack and data files. All figures traced to source. Currency: AED mn unless noted. Fiscal years 2017–2021 (actuals); Euromonitor market data 2017–2026 (USD mn, retail value RSP)._

---

## 0. Client goal (from the brief)

Dairyworks — a UAE-based dairy company (founded 1990; milk, yogurt, ice cream; mass-market) — has a **new CEO** and **declining performance**. The CEO wants a **5-year value creation plan**:

1. **Full-potential revenue and EBITDA**
2. **Strategic initiatives** for profitability optimization and growth
3. Grounded in an assessment of **current state** (financials, portfolio, footprint, channels), **market outlook**, and **competitive position**.

So-what lens for every finding: _what does this mean for the CEO's value creation decision?_

---

## 1. The headline: value has collapsed in a growing market

| Metric | 2017 | 2021 | CAGR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Volume (000' tons) | 313.1 | 271.5 | **−3.5%** |
| Revenue (AED mn) | 2,000 | 1,900 | **−1.3%** |
| EBITDA (AED mn) | 362 | 111 | **−25.6%** |
| EBITDA margin | 18.1% | **5.8%** | −12.3pp |

- EBITDA fell **69%** (AED 362M → 111M) in four years; margin lost **12.3pp**.
- Volume is shrinking **−3.5%/yr** while realized price rose **+2.3%/yr** (AED 6.39k → 7.00k per ton) — Dairyworks has been **pricing up a declining-volume, weak-brand business** and losing share.
- **Critical context:** its markets are *growing* 5–8%/yr (Section 4). The decline is **self-inflicted and fixable**, not structural.

## 2. Diagnosis A — where the 12.3pp of margin went (cost walk, % of revenue)

| Line | 2017 | 2021 | Δ (pp) | Driver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| COGS | 59.3% | 64.5% | **+5.2** | Outdated plants, no scale economies, procurement |
| S&D | 17.0% | 24.9% | **+7.9** | Distribution dysfunction across all 3 countries (biggest drag) |
| A&M | 0.6% | 0.6% | 0.0 | Chronic brand underinvestment (peers spend 2–3%) |
| G&A | 5.0% | 4.2% | −0.8 | Modest efficiency |
| **EBITDA** | **18.1%** | **5.8%** | **−12.3** | |

- **S&D (+7.9pp) and COGS (+5.2pp) explain the entire collapse.** Both are operational and controllable.
- A&M starvation (0.6% vs peers' 2–3%) directly explains the **brand crisis**: only **1 in 5** consumers recognize Dairyworks, **~60%** of those don't understand the brand, only **1 in 10** seek it out (2019 Nielsen, n=1,000).

## 3. Diagnosis B — portfolio: the commodity core is melting, premium/health is winning

| Product | Rev 2017 | Rev 2021 | Rev CAGR | GP% 2017 | GP% 2021 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard milk, 1L | 800 | 570 | **−8.1%** | 40% | **30%** |
| Organic milk, 1L | 360 | 513 | **+9.3%** | 40% | 40% |
| Standard yogurt, 500g | 340 | 285 | −4.3% | 40% | 30% |
| Flavored yogurt, 120g | 160 | 304 | **+17.4%** | 40% | 40% |
| Ice cream tub, 1L | 340 | 228 | −9.5% | 44% | 40% |
| **Total** | **2,000** | **1,900** | −1.3% | 41% | 36% |

- **Winners** are premium/health: organic milk (+9.3%) and flavored yogurt (+17.4%) — both hold 40% GP. Together still only **~43%** of revenue → under-scaled.
- **Losers** are commodity: standard milk (−8.1%, GP 40%→30%), ice cream (−9.5%), standard yogurt (−4.3%). GP compression is concentrated in the scale-/cost-disadvantaged commodity lines.
- By geography: **UAE** −3.0% (home market eroding), **KSA** +1.5% (only ~2% share → biggest headroom), **Qatar** +0.8%.

## 4. Diagnosis C — market is a tailwind, not a headwind (Euromonitor, USD mn RSP)

Core addressable market (milk + organic milk + plain & flavored yogurt + ice cream):

| Market | 2021 | 2026E | CAGR 21–26 | Dairyworks share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UAE | 695 | 984 | **+7.2%** | ~15% |
| KSA | 1,790 | 2,319 | **+5.3%** | ~2% |
| Qatar | 255 | 373 | **+7.9%** | ~7% |

High-growth pockets (CAGR 21–26): **UAE organic milk +11.8%, plant-based milk +9.9% (Qatar +13.6%), flavored yogurt +7–9%, sour-milk/health +8.5%**. Dairyworks is losing share in categories that are compounding — the single clearest proof the problem is internal.

## 5. Diagnosis D — competitive gap is operational, not inevitable (2021, % of revenue)

| | COGS | S&D | A&M | G&A | **EBITDA** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dairyworks** | 64.5% | 24.9% | 0.6% | 4.2% | **5.8%** |
| Almarai | 55.8% | 14.1% | 2.0% | 1.1% | **27.0%** |
| NADEC | 57.0% | 18.0% | 2.8% | 3.0% | **19.2%** |
| SADAFCO | 64.0% | 14.2% | 2.2% | 2.6% | **17.0%** |

- Dairyworks' EBITDA margin is **~11–21pp below peers**. The gap is almost entirely **S&D (+7–11pp worse)** and **inverted A&M** (it under-invests in brand while over-spending on distribution).
- SADAFCO proves 17% EBITDA is achievable at similar COGS — the prize is in **distribution and brand**, not just the factory.

## 6. Synthesis — the "one thing"

> **Dairyworks' collapse is self-inflicted and reversible: margins fell 12pp in markets growing 5–8%. Fix distribution and cost, reinvest in brand, and tilt the portfolio to premium/health to roughly 4.5x EBITDA to ~AED 495M by Year 5.**

## 7. Full-potential value creation plan (5-year)

**Revenue bridge (AED mn):** 1,900 → **2,700** (CAGR +7.3%, in line with market)

| Lever | Uplift |
|---|---|
| Distribution reset — regain shelf & share in KSA/Qatar (fix outsourced networks) | +260 |
| Premiumization — scale organic milk & flavored yogurt | +300 |
| Core defense + modern/online channel growth (UAE) | +180 |
| Adjacencies — plant-based & functional dairy entry | +60 |

**EBITDA margin bridge (pp):** 5.8% → **18.3%**

| Lever | Δ (pp) |
|---|---|
| Distribution / S&D turnaround (24.9%→16.5%) | +8.4 |
| Manufacturing modernization + procurement scale (COGS 64.5%→60.0%) | +4.5 |
| Portfolio mix / premiumization (GP uplift) | +1.5 |
| Brand reinvestment (A&M 0.6%→2.5%) | −1.9 |

**Full potential (Year 5): Revenue ~AED 2,700M, EBITDA ~AED 495M (18.3%) — +AED 384M EBITDA, ~4.5x 2021.**

### Four strategic initiatives
1. **Distribution turnaround** — insource/renegotiate KSA & Qatar, rebuild UAE route-to-market, win back shelf. (Largest single EBITDA lever.)
2. **Cost & manufacturing modernization** — upgrade the 3 UAE plants, centralize procurement, capture scale. 
3. **Portfolio premiumization** — scale organic milk & flavored yogurt; rationalize loss-making commodity SKUs; enter plant-based/functional.
4. **Brand & demand reset** — step up A&M from 0.6%→2.5% to fix the 1-in-5 awareness problem and pull premium volume.

### Roadmap (3 horizons)
- **H1 (0–12 mo): Stop the bleed** — distribution renegotiation, procurement quick wins, SKU rationalization, brand relaunch brief.
- **H2 (12–36 mo): Build the engine** — plant modernization capex, premium portfolio scale-up, KSA share offensive, brand campaign.
- **H3 (36–60 mo): Full potential** — adjacencies (plant-based/functional), channel/online leadership, EBITDA to ~18%.

---

_Assumptions & caveats: Revenue uplifts are initiative-based and additive to a 2021 base; they assume share recovery toward market growth and successful distribution/brand execution. Margin bridge targets peer-median economics (SADAFCO/NADEC) rather than best-in-class Almarai. Capex and working-capital needs to be sized in due diligence. Market data is Euromonitor RSP (retail sell-out), used for growth/direction, not as Dairyworks' own revenue base._
