2Strategic context & problem statement
Current situation / pain point
Every commodity SKU carries the identical list price in 2017 and 2021. Five years of input-cost inflation was absorbed into margin — commodity GP% fell ~40%→30%. Net price is further diluted by a 24.9%-of-revenue S&D / trade line with unmanaged promotions.
Target future state & scope
Phased list-price recovery across standard milk, yogurt & ice cream to rebuild GP toward 40%; price-pack architecture (entry / core / premium, smaller packs at higher price/kg); trade-spend reset managing net price. Scope: UAE domestic commodity portfolio (export pricing sits in Initiative 5).
3Financial & operational business case
Baseline metric
Commodity GP ~30% · price flat 5 yrs
Target metric
Commodity GP ~38–40% · +7–17%/line
Run-rate net benefit
~AED 200M / yr (recurring)
Cost-to-achieve
Opex ~AED 2–4M · minimal capex
Net Year 1
~AED 60–90M in-year (phased)
Impact type
Recurring
Calculation logic & key assumptions
  • Formula: Δprice% × recoverable volume base (~190 kt commodity) → revenue; GP uplift ≈ price Δ less variable cost.
  • Assumption 1: phased single-to-mid-teens increases hold ~90% of volume (volume lost to elasticity saves its own variable cost).
  • Assumption 2: trade-spend reset recovers ~2–3pp of net price without distribution loss.

Leading: # SKUs repriced · promo-ROI approvals · price-list go-live %.
Lagging: realised net price vs baseline · commodity GP%.
Sustained: GP% ≥38% held 2 consecutive quarters · price-pack compliance >95%.

Local capex only after proof. Best practice: Almarai scaled via distributor-led export across GCC / MENA.
