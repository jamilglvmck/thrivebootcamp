What the benchmark tells us
  • S&D is the smoking gun: +7.9pp while every peer held or improved — part inefficiency (outsourced KSA/Qatar), part deleverage on falling volume
  • COGS is mostly exogenous: every peer's COGS rose too (Almarai +10.3pp, worse than us) — global dairy-input & energy inflation; our controllable share is scale/plants
  • A&M inverted: we starve demand (0.6%) while over-paying to supply
So what: The addressable prize is S&D first, then scale-driven COGS — not price. ~half the 12.3pp loss is self-inflicted and fully fixable.

Revenue is weak for two reasons — we are losing share behind a weak brand, and our mix is drifting the wrong way

Volume, share, brand and product mix, 2017–2021
1 · DECLINING SHARE & WEAK BRAND
  • Volume −13% (313→272kt) while markets grew ~5–8%/yr — price (+10%) masked a real volume/share loss
  • Losing share where it counts: UAE milk and ice cream (our home market is −3%/yr)
  • Weak brand (2019 Nielsen): only 1-in-5 recognize us, 1-in-10 actively seek us — demand isn't being pulled
