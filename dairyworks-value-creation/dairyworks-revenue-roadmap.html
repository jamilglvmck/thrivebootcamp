Value creation › Lever 2 of 5 · Share recovery (distribution & brand)

Lever 2 — We shrank while the market grew; closing the volume gap through distribution and brand is the single largest pool

Four gaps opened up while the market moved on — each one is ours to close
Where we lost ground
The market grew — we shrank
Milk, yogurt and ice cream all kept expanding, yet our volumes fell. We handed over share, year after year.
We slipped off the shelf
Distribution reach and on-shelf availability eroded — shoppers simply can't buy a product they can't find.
The brand runs on empty
Marketing is a fraction of what peers spend and few shoppers recognise us — there is nothing pulling demand through.
We gave up export shelf
Listings surrendered in KSA and Qatar handed distribution — and the shopper relationship — straight to competitors.
What we will do
  • Rebuild UAE route-to-market — fix on-shelf availability and distribution reach (the core of the S&D problem)
  • Step up brand investment to peer levels and relaunch to lift recognition and pull demand
  • Win back KSA & Qatar listings — regain distribution lost in export markets
  • Optimise channels — modern-trade execution plus e-/quick-commerce, where share is won cheaply
+~AED 250M revenue
Largest pool; needs the distribution fix and brand investment to land — build through Horizons 1–2.
So what: Simply growing with the market — not even gaining share — recovers the biggest slice of lost revenue; distribution and brand are the mechanism.
Source: Dairyworks product & geography volumes (2017–2021); Euromonitor UAE retail value; 2019 Nielsen brand recognition; peer A&M benchmark.

All four grow faster than our current categories — and we have zero presence. Existing chilled distribution and supply chain make entry low-incremental-cost.
Best practice — proven by other brands
Oatly — plant-based
Bold rebrand + taste-led, packaging-as-media → +700% demand, 21% CAGR. Lesson: lead on taste, not just health.
Chobani — protein / Greek yogurt
Omnichannel + sampling → +8pt awareness, 51% new buyers, $10.60 ROAS. Lesson: recruit new households via retail media & trial.
Halo Top — better-for-you ice cream
Lead-retailer launch + in-store sampling → #3 UK brand within 12 months. Lesson: anchor on one retailer, drive trial.
The playbook for us: enter on a clear better-for-you proposition, lead with taste, launch with a lead retailer + sampling, and amplify through online / quick-commerce.
So what: The adjacencies are large and fast-growing, and the winning formula is already documented — we can follow a proven path rather than invent one. Value captured: +~AED 110M.
Source: Euromonitor UAE retail value (2021–26E). Best-practice results: Oatly / Forsman & Bodenfors; Chobani / Walmart Connect & Snapchat; Halo Top / dunnhumby & Shopkick case studies.
