Value creation › Lever 1 of 5 · Pricing & revenue management

Lever 1 — Price has been flat for five years; a modest rise restores commodity margin

The pricing solutions, and the revenue and margin they unlock
The solutions
1 · Recover list price
What: Phase modest increases — single-digit to mid-teens — across commodity lines.
Why: Price frozen five years; cost inflation cut commodity GP from 40%→30% — pure recoverable margin.
2 · Price-pack architecture
What: Entry, core and premium price points; smaller packs at a higher price per kg.
Why: Lifts effective price without sticker shock, and keeps an opening price point for value shoppers.
3 · Premium price ladders
What: Raise prices on organic and flavoured lines.
Why: They already grow double-digit at 40% GP — willingness-to-pay is proven, so the headroom is real.
The impact

Value creation › Lever 2 of 5 · Share recovery (distribution & brand)

Lever 2 — We shrank while the market grew; closing the gap is the biggest prize

We shrank while the market grew — distribution and brand win it back
The solutions
1 · Rebuild route-to-market
What: Fix on-shelf availability, distribution reach and modern-trade + online execution.
Why: Availability slipped while the market grew — shoppers can't buy what they can't find.
2 · Relaunch the brand
What: Step up marketing to peer levels and relaunch to lift recognition.
Why: Spend is a fraction of peers and few shoppers recognise us — nothing pulls demand.
3 · Win back export listings
What: Regain the KSA & Qatar listings we lost.
Why: We handed shelf — and the shopper relationship — straight to competitors.

Value creation › Lever 3 of 5 · Mix & premiumization

Lever 3 — Shift the mix to premium; it lifts revenue and margin at once

Premium already grew from 26% to 43% of revenue — push it further
The solutions
1 · Scale the premium winners
What: Put volume, marketing and shelf behind organic milk & flavoured yogurt.
Why: They grow double-digit at 40% margin — the mix already shifts our way.
2 · Premiumize & relaunch ice cream
What: Re-platform our highest-margin category and relaunch it premium.
Why: It earns 40% margin yet is declining fastest — the biggest self-help gap.
3 · Add premium formats
What: Launch high-protein, single-serve and gifting packs.
Why: New formats recruit new shoppers at premium price points.

Value creation › Lever 4 of 5 · New categories

Lever 4 — Enter fast-growing categories we are missing, using a proven playbook

What has worked for other brands — and the adjacencies to copy it into
Best practice — proven by other brands
Oatly — plant-based
Bold rebrand + taste-led, packaging-as-media → +700% demand, 21% CAGR. Lesson: lead on taste, not just health.
Chobani — protein / Greek yogurt
Omnichannel + sampling → +8pt awareness, 51% new buyers, $10.60 ROAS. Lesson: recruit new households via retail media & trial.
Halo Top — better-for-you ice cream
Lead-retailer launch + in-store sampling → #3 UK brand within 12 months. Lesson: anchor on one retailer, drive trial.
The playbook for us: enter on a clear better-for-you proposition, lead with taste, launch with a lead retailer + sampling, and amplify through online / quick-commerce.
