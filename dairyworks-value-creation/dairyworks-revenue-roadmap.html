Price per SKU is identical in 2017 and 2021 — every dirham of decline is lost volume.
  • Price frozen for 5 years. Every SKU holds its 2017 price; on commodity lines cost inflation cut GP% from 40%→30% — pricing/revenue-management is untapped and pure margin.
  • Volume lost to the market. UAE volume fell ~5%/yr in milk and ~13%/yr in ice cream while the market grew ~4%/yr — share recovery (distribution + brand) is the largest pool.
  • Mix is already winning. Premium rose 26%→43% of revenue at ~+12%/yr and holds 40% GP — accelerate premiumization, manage commodity for cash, enter adjacencies (plant-based +10%/yr, sour milk +8.5%/yr).
Note: Premium = organic milk + flavoured yogurt; Commodity = standard milk, standard yogurt, ice cream. Price = revenue ÷ volume per SKU. CAGR = compound annual growth rate 2017–21.
